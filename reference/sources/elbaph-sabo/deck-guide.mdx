## Thresholds that change the board

- **4+ Life:** Sabo is a 4K Leader. **[Card text]**
- **1 DON!! + an 8+ cost Character:** Sabo and your Characters gain +1K. **[Card text]**
- **Any 12+ cost Character:** the 2-cost Elbaph Straw Hats gain +3K. This can be your Character or the opponent's. **[Card text]**
- **Saul:** printed cost 4, but gains +12 cost and becomes cost 16 for the relevant threshold. **[Card text]**
- **Loki:** printed cost 6, but gains +12 cost and becomes cost 18 for the relevant threshold. **[Card text]**
- **Pirates Docking Six:** becomes cost 11. It activates Sabo's 8+ condition, but it does **not** turn on the 12+ effects. **[Card text]**
- **Monkey.D.Luffy:** cost 8 activates Sabo. Its Rush line needs a 12+ cost Character, and it can replay a 2-cost Character. **[Card text]**

## Core loop

1. **Fix the hand.** Nami, Usopp, and Saul find the pieces; Nami can turn an unwanted Elbaph card into two draws. **[Presenter claim]**
2. **Build wide.** The 2-cost Straw Hats are not only setup cards: beside a 12+ cost Character they become 6K bodies after Sabo's +1K. **[Card text]**
3. **Choose the activator.** Saul or Loki turns on the 12+ board threshold. Docking Six is a replay engine, but it is only cost 11. **[Card text]**
4. **Convert the board.** Robin supplies -3K, Zoro protects the board, Jinbe blocks, and Chopper makes a 12+ board dangerous to leave at zero Life. **[Presenter claim]**
5. **Close with Luffy.** With a 12+ cost Character established, Luffy can Rush and replay the 2-cost body that matches the board. **[Card text]**

## Decision cards

### Saul vs Loki

- **Saul:** the presenter calls it one of the most important cards and a reliable 12+ activator. **[Presenter claim]**
- **Loki:** a large attacker with an opponent-turn power boost and a potential 4-cost-or-less KO. It can also activate Red Ace's 10-cost Newgate, so the presenter warns against dropping it carelessly in that matchup. **[Presenter claim]**

### Docking Six vs Luffy

- **Pirates Docking Six:** five DON!! for a replay line and top-three trashing; cost 11 means it does not activate the 12+ effects. **[Card text / Presenter claim]**
- **Luffy:** the closer. Cost 8 activates Sabo, and with a 12+ Character it can Rush and replay a 2-cost. **[Card text]**

### Robin vs Kong Gun

- **Robin:** two DON!! for -3K can make an existing attack become removal. **[Presenter claim]**
- **Gum-Gum Kong Gun:** a 3K counter, plus a six-DON!! line to KO two opposing 6-cost-or-less Characters when a 12+ Character is present. The presenter recommends it when you already have the board and two good targets. **[Card text / Presenter claim]**
